Sermon Bot

Interact with Pastor Chris' preaching using AI.

Thirty years of sermons is a lot to dig through. Sermon Bot lets you just ask. Wondering what I've taught about anxiety, marriage, suffering, money, prayer, or forgiveness? Ask in plain words and it'll draw from my actual preaching, point you to the specific sermons, and give you the links to go listen or read.

It can also answer everyday questions about Providence Community Church — service times, location, what to expect on a Sunday — and help you get in touch. It's an AI assistant, so it sticks to what the church and my sermons actually say, and it hands you off to a real person whenever that's the better next step.

Or tap the green chat button in the bottom-right corner anytime.
