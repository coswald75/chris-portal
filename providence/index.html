Providence Community Church

“Truth and Beauty in Community.”

Whatever else my wife and I have going on in life, we are thrilled to devote most of our energy toward the care and cultivation of Providence Community Church. Alongside the rest of the leadership team, we are happy to serve Jesus in the midst of this sweet congregation — a people seeking to exemplify “Truth and Beauty in Community.”

Providence is a gospel-centered, Reformed church in Lenexa, Kansas, and part of the Sovereign Grace family of churches. We gather around the ordinary means of grace — the Word preached, the sacraments, and prayer — and we share life together as a community shaped by the gospel. Whatever your story, you are welcome here.
