The Mission-Driven Marriage

Biblical teaching about marriage you might be missing.

Most of us were handed a vision of marriage built mainly around personal happiness — compatibility, romance, and a partner who completes us. Scripture tells a larger and more compelling story. Marriage is mission-driven: a covenant union aimed at God's glory and the good of others, not merely our own fulfillment. When a husband and wife understand that their marriage exists for something bigger than themselves, even the ordinary days take on weight and purpose.

This project is where I'm working to recover that bigger picture — the biblical teaching about marriage that many of us were never taught, and that has the power to reframe everything from how we handle conflict to how we order our home, our calling, and our witness.
