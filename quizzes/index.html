Quizzes for Christians

Test yourself, find your weak spots, and get a plan to fix them.

  • Bible Knowledge Exam

    100 questions on the people, places, books, sayings, doctrine, and storyline of Scripture. Your progress saves as you go, and when you finish you'll get your score, the answers you missed, and a personalized two-week study plan aimed at your weakest areas.

  • Self-Sabotage Stopper

    Calamities, shipwrecked faith, and pastoral self-immolations feel like they're at an all-time high. There are warning signs. Take the test! Eight short integrity self-checks for pastors — your answers are scored and discarded, never stored.

  • Marriage Counseling Intake

    For couples beginning marital counseling with Pastor Chris. Each spouse fills out their own form — log in with your email and a password, save as you go, and your completed form is delivered directly and only to Pastor Chris.

  • Suffering Sheets

    Ten biblical counseling worksheets for walking with God through suffering — lament, prayer, endurance, and hope, one sheet at a time. What you write stays on your device, with the option to share a finished sheet with Pastor Chris.

  • When Silence is Sin

    A self-examination worksheet on speaking the truth in love — for when the fear of someone's reaction tempts us to stay silent. Scripture study, self-examination, repentance, and a concrete plan to "say the thing." What you write stays on your device, with the option to share it with Pastor Chris.
