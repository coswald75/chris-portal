Quizzes for Christians

Worksheets and tests for growth and grace.

A growing collection of quizzes, worksheets, and short tests designed to help Christians take honest stock of their walk with Christ — not to measure up to a standard, but to grow in grace. Good questions have a way of surfacing what a sermon or a book can leave buried, and these are built to do exactly that.

Use them on your own, with a spouse or friend, or in a discipleship or small-group setting.
