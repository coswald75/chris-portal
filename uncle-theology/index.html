Uncle Theology

Songs that teach.

While music can be used to entertain and distract, it has always been used to teach. Thanks to AI tools like Suno, guys like me — with a fair bit of theological knowledge and a penchant for industrial-grade poetry — can produce songs that teach.

The heart of this project is my desire to equip my nieces and nephews with essential truths, so every month I try to ship a new album, posted along with scripture references and questions for contemplation. It's catechesis with a beat — truth set to music that's worth hearing more than once.
