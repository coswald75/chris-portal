Arts & Letters

Making, and the reading that feeds it — poems, pictures, music, and the long conversation of the books.

The reading lives here as the Great Ideas: one idea a night, traced through the Scriptures, the Greeks, the fathers and schoolmen, the essayists and poets — all read from the public domain, closed with a hymn. It walks Mortimer Adler’s hundred and two Great Ideas in his own order, Angel to World, with Adler as the mapmaker and never the source. These pages are drawn from the Vespers project; the chart below is printed whole, and the links are the nights written so far.
