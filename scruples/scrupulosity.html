Scrupulosity is the Law of Performance running inside a single conscience. Everything else on this wiki is cosmic and civilisational; this is what it looks like at one square foot of pew. It is the argument's proof of life.

Paul makes the turn himself

“Do not handle, do not taste, do not touch” (Col 2:21) sits three verses after “if with Christ you died to the stoicheia.” The classic text on scrupulosity is the classic text on the elemental principles. The cosmic argument of vv. 8–15 lands, in Paul's own hands, on somebody being made to feel disqualified over a festival and a diet.

Paul's diagnosis is not that the scrupulous person is trying too hard. It is that the system they are serving cannot deliver what it advertises — an appearance of wisdom, and no value against the flesh.

Five headwaters

The pastoral commitment of the whole treatment is anti-monocausal: scrupulosity has several sources and they require different responses. Three of the five plug into the stoicheia frame. Two do not, and protecting that limit matters more than the connection does.

Pastoral register. Some people in the room are fighting a brain, not only a lie. Framing scrupulosity as occupation without qualification hands an already-tormented person one more thing to be terrified of, and makes compulsions into a spiritual failure. The gospel speaks to them without that.

The King's cubit

Every measuring rod drifts, so a standard is kept outside the marketplace and rods are calibrated against it. Stoicheia are drifted rods that have set themselves up as the standard — which is exactly the register of the word, since it meant rudiments, the basic series, the row of letters.

Col 2:17 supplies the calibration language directly: shadow against substance, and the substance belongs to Christ. Christ is the King's cubit.

What the sermon left on the table

The preached version quotes v. 20 and then takes its three observations from vv. 21–23. The clause carrying the cosmic argument is read and never touched; “elemental spirits” appears once, unexplained. Three costs:

  • The accuser stream is asserted, not grounded. It never says why he has already lost. That is six verses up.
  • “You died to” does unexplained work. Calibration and redirection are both technique. Paul's remedy is ontological: you are not fighting the system, you are dead to it.
  • The Ecclesiastes landing is a verdict without a ground. God has already approved — why? Col 2:14. The verdict is announced and the courtroom skipped.

The counter-argument for restraint is real: a topical study already carrying five streams at thirty-two minutes cannot absorb a cosmic-powers excursus, and pivoting from seed oils to principalities is a genre collision. The fix is about ninety seconds inside the accuser stream, not a new section — and since the Advent 2025 sermon already taught the room the word, it costs a callback rather than a lecture.

The Advent hook

A scrupulous conscience is still keeping Advent after Christmas: still waiting for a verdict that has already been announced. Christ was born under the stoicheia (Gal 4:4) so that the waiting could end.
