What is not stoicheia

ArticleTalkReadEditNotesHistory
BoundaryThis page exists so the limit has an address instead of living in footnotes.

The stoicheia frame is totalising by nature. It wants to explain everything, and it is good enough at explaining things that it usually can — which is the danger. A framework that accounts for every case has stopped making claims and started absorbing them.

This page is the standing correction. Everything listed here is not to be explained by the stoicheia, and linking it to the hub is an error even when it looks tidy.

Not stoicheia

A tender conscience

On Sibbes's reading, tenderness can be a sign of good soil rather than of occupation. If scrupulosity can indicate health, it cannot be simply the enemy's work — and saying otherwise contradicts the source the argument leans on.

A sick brain

Scrupulosity is frequently an OCD presentation. Compulsions are not a spiritual failure and intrusive thoughts are not a tenant. Treating them as such is both false and cruel, and it is the failure mode most likely to reach someone in real distress.

Ordinary grief, and most suffering

The performance regime reads suffering as verdict. The correction has to run all the way: not everything painful is diagnostic of anything. John 9:3 declines the question rather than answering it with a better cause.

Creation, and the structures themselves

The crab is not the shell. Marriage, money, government and conscience are good, and calling a structure “a stoicheia” as a synonym for “bad” collapses the distinction the whole framework depends on.

Disagreement

People who differ on secondary matters are not thereby running a performance regime. The accusation “that's just stoicheia” is itself a fast way to build a new wall, since it ranks people by how free they are.

A usable test

Before linking anything here, three questions:

  1. Is there an audience? A performance regime always has one. If no one is being performed for, this is something else.
  2. Is worth being assigned? Not merely rules — rules are fine — but rules that decide who counts.
  3. Would naming it help this person, or only tidy the diagram? If it only tidies the diagram, do not say it from a pulpit.
Editorial rule. If a page cannot answer all three, it does not get a instantiates link to the hub. It may still exist. It may still be related. It is not a case of this.
