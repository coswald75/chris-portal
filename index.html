The Shepherds Guild

A big part of my ministry burden has to do with helping pastors serve their local churches with joy, backbone, and excellence. The Shepherds Guild is the culmination of 30 years of thinking about the nature of preaching. It decomposes a pastor's own sermon archive into a rhetorical profile — theological emphases, illustration patterns, a preaching fingerprint — and sets it alongside the masters so preachers can grow in their own voice.

theshepherdsguild.com

Uncle Theology

While music can be used to entertain and distract, it has always been used to teach. Thanks to AI tools like Suno, guys like me — with a fair bit of theological knowledge and a penchant for poetry — can produce songs that teach. The heart of this project is my desire to equip my nieces and nephews with essential truths, so every month I try to ship a new album, posted along with scripture references and questions for contemplation.
