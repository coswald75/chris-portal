Reading Isaiah · §18

The Chemistry of Nations

Numbers 23:9 · Judges 11:24 · Psalm 96:5 · Nations have to cooperate. Trade has to cross borders, wells have to be shared, wars have to end in something. The ancient world had a working technology for all of it — and the covenant handed Israel a confession that broke the technology on purpose. This page explains the machine the next three pages watch Israel's kings misuse — and adds the third exposure to the series' diagram: what happens when triangles touch.

Where the chemistry is headed

Hold the picture through the three case files, because the gospel does not end it — it completes it. The new covenant people are exactly as inert, sideways, as Israel ever was: no god-exchange, no oaths under foreign witnesses, no treaty-marriages. What changes is the bonding model itself — the day is coming when the nations stop bonding to each other god-to-god and every triangle bonds instead to one Center, "in whom all things hold together." Act IV draws that molecule. First, the forgeries.
