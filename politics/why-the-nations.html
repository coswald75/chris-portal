Reading Isaiah · §3

Why the Nations?

Isaiah 2:1–4 · Genesis 12:1–3 · The last two pages built the lens: a nation is a family, under a god, on a land, embodied in a king. This page supplies the motive — the case that God's purpose for Israel was never Israel alone, traced from Abraham's call to the last page of Revelation. Everything the coming weeks do with Moab, Egypt, and Babylon serves this one argument; none of it competes with it.

The whole argument, in one line

Genesis 12 promises it. Exodus 9 and 19 state it as the reason for everything that follows. Isaiah spends thirty chapters proving God means it, nation by nation. Matthew 28 and Acts repeat it as marching orders, in Isaiah's own words. Revelation shows it finished. Five books, one purpose, and this series lives entirely inside the middle of it.
