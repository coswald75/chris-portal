Reading Isaiah · §23

A New Covenant People

1 Peter 2:9–10 · Psalm 45 · Isaiah 56 · Revelation 21:24 · The series taught a triangle: a nation is a family, under a god, on a land, embodied in a king. Now the question the whole New Testament forces: what kind of nation is the church? Peter answers in the triad's own grammar — every corner kept, every corner transposed.

The triangle, kept and transfigured

Isaiah's definition of a nation survives all the way to the end — family, worship, inheritance, King. What the gospel abolishes is not nationhood but the two lies that corrupted every triangle on the map: the god who wasn't there, and the treaty that relocated trust. One page remains: John's vision of the finished gathering — the nations walking in the light of the city whose lamp is the Lamb.
