Reading Isaiah · §21

A Covenant with Death

Isaiah 28:14–22 · 30:1–17 · 31:1–3 · The third instrument: the secret treaty. A generation after Ahaz, Jerusalem's operators cut a deal with Egypt against Assyria — and Isaiah refuses to call it diplomacy. He gives the treaty its true legal name, and sets against it the only architecture that survives the storm.

The third instrument, audited — and the act closed

Three instruments, one sin in three costumes: the marriage that moved the corners, the oath that re-filed the sonship, the secret pact that contracted with Death — each a relocation of trust from the God above the triangle to another triangle's king. Against all three, one alternative, stated in stone: whoever believes will not be in haste. Act IV asks what happened when the tested stone became a Man, and the nations' triangles met their true Center.
