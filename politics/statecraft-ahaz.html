Reading Isaiah · §20

Servant and Son

2 Kings 16 · Isaiah 7–8 · 2 Chronicles 28 · The second instrument of statecraft: the vassal oath. Under siege by the coalition, offered any sign in heaven or Sheol, Ahaz instead mails two words to Nineveh — and the study watches what those two words cost each corner of Judah's triangle.

The second instrument, audited

The vassal oath moved every corner in order: sonship re-filed (relation), the altar imported (religion), the land shaved to stubble (region) — the full triangle, surrendered for a rescue that arrived as a razor. One instrument remains: the secret treaty a later generation will cut with Egypt, which Isaiah refuses to call diplomacy at all. He calls it a covenant with death.
