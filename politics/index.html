Politics

How people order their common life — authority, justice, the two cities and their loves.

Two tracks run through this locus. Themes in political theology gather the argument as it has been worked out from the pulpit — the reign of Christ, the two cities, authority and its limits, the Exodus, the public square, wealth, and mission — each paired, where one exists, with a companion essay. Reading Isaiah: the Nations is the running commentary underneath them — the prophet’s oracles against the peoples, and what they teach about power, idols, and the Lord who rules the nations.
