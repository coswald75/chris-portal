AI Assistant

Learn more about Pastor Chris's preaching, the church, and inquire about in-person meetings.

Thirty years of sermons is a lot to dig through. This assistant lets you just ask. Wondering what I've taught about anxiety, marriage, suffering, money, prayer, or forgiveness? Ask in plain words and it'll draw from my actual preaching, point you to the specific sermons, and give you the links to go listen or read.

It can also answer everyday questions about Providence Community Church — service times, location, what to expect on a Sunday — and, if you'd like to meet in person, it can show you my open times and set up a request. It's an AI assistant, so it sticks to what the church and my sermons actually say, and it hands you off to a real person whenever that's the better next step.

Or tap the green chat button in the bottom-right corner anytime.
