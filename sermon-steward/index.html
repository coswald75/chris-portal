Sermon Steward

Sunday's sermon, stewarded and shipped.

Sermon Steward is step one in building what I hope will become an obviously superior sermon hosting and dissemination platform. I don't know of anything else like it available to local churches — in my opinion, everybody should be using it.

Built by pastors, for pastors, it takes the sermon a church has already labored over on Sunday and stewards it well: hosting it, shaping it, and shipping it out in the formats a congregation can actually use through the week and beyond. A sermon is too costly a thing to preach once and lose. Sermon Steward exists to make sure it keeps working long after the benediction.
