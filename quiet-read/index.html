The Quiet Read App

Read anything, one sentence at a time.

A distraction-free reader that lets you read anything one sentence at a time. I built it for myself, found it so useful that I wanted to share it with others — your readings and saved passages, on every device.

Paste in a passage, import a PDF, or pull up a chapter of Scripture, and the app serves it to you one sentence at a time, stripping away the clutter that pulls your attention apart. Anything worth keeping goes into a personal commonplace collection you can return to later. It is a small tool with a simple aim: to help you slow down and actually take in what you're reading.
