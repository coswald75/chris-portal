Madon vs. Shalom — The Master Table

The synthesis page of Litigating Without a License: the whole project in two columns. Every row is developed on its home page (linked in the left column). Read down the middle column and you have the anatomy of the critical spirit; read down the right and you have the anatomy of the settled soul. Every human being is running one of these two operating systems in every relationship, and most of us run different ones in different rooms.

Dimension MADON — the open ledger SHALOM — the settled account
The word (Madon / Shalom) מָדוֹן, from din: litigation-shaped strife — the case perpetually pressed שָׁלוֹם, from shalem/shillem: wholeness as paid-up-ness — nothing outstanding
The patron (Diabolos — The Accuser / The Advocate (Paraklētos)) The katēgoros — accusing day and night (Rev 12:10) The paraklētos — interceding always (Heb 7:25; Rom 8:34)
The docket (Diabolos — The Accuser / Din) Reopens cases God has closed (Rom 8:33 defied) The closed docket: "It is God who justifies"
Courtroom role (Din) Volunteer prosecutor — litigating without a license Junior counsel for the defense — the only licensed practice
The inner court (The Internal Courtroom) The silent tribunal: verdicts before words (Simon, Michal) Court adjourned; peace seated as umpire (Col 3:15, brabeuetō)
Inner speech (The Internal Courtroom) Rehearsing the prosecution ("said to himself") Preaching to yourself (Ps 42:5; 103:1–2)
The record (Ingratitude) Rigged: benefits deleted, gifts re-attributed (Deut 8:17; Naomi's "empty") Honest discovery: benefits recited, couriers named, the chief exhibit kept on the table (Eucharist)
Epistemology (Slander, Gossip, and the Busybody / <a class="wl" href="madon-and-the-anxious-tone-setter.html" title="Friedman found that institutions herd behind their most anxious member; Numbers 13–14 shows what that is theologically — an Accusing God evil report about providence given the gavel — and the parable of the talents prices it: safety-first is a verdict about the Master, the buried">Madon and the Anxious Tone-Setter) The epistemology of pride: all the data, one interpretation, my verdict
Verdict style (Slander, Gossip, and the Busybody) Data-defensible but false ("you never..."; the dibbah) Love's construal: believes all things, holds even that provisionally (1 Cor 13:7)
Speech traffic (Slander, Gossip, and the Busybody / The Advocate (Paraklētos)) The pipeline: busybody → whisper → slander (verdicts in circulation) Paraclesis: encouragement daily (Heb 3:13), evidences of grace named, blessing circulating
Case about a person (Riv) Filed in the kitchen, the group chat, the heart Filed at the bench: intercession as the licensed form of having a case (1 John 5:16)
Verdict on God (Accusing God) The hard master (Matt 25:24); the suit against providence in worry, anxiety, fear "He exists and rewards those who seek him" (Heb 11:6); sovereign and good (Ps 119:68)
Under trial (Accusing God / Shalom) Grumbling in the tents (Num 14; Deut 1:27) Lament at the bench (Jer 12:1) — and "count it all joy" (James 1:2)
The engine — hers (Eshet Midyanim) Fear executing its verdict through management of people Hope in God; the quiet spirit; won-without-a-word power (1 Pet 3)
The engine — his (Ish Midyanim) Threatened glory: pride and envy prosecuting rivals (1 Tim 6:4–5) Glory secured in Christ; honor's only lawful race — "outdo one another in showing honor" (Rom 12:10)
Desire (Madon and Mimesis) Borrowed: routed through the neighbor (Eccl 4:4; the tenth commandment's target) Discipled: "Imitate me as I imitate Christ" — the only non-rival Model (Phil 2:6)
Economics (Madon and Mimesis) Zero-sum theology: fixed pie, Malthusian math, the buried talent Generative: loaves multiply, "all that is mine is yours," the cup overflows (Ps 23:5)
A rival's increase (Madon and Mimesis) "Saul eyed David from that day" (1 Sam 18:9) "He must increase... this joy of mine is now complete" (John 3:29–30)
Power and station (Madon and Radical Egalitarianism) Disputing the disbursement: Korah's syllogism, Absalom's kiss, the hermeneutic of accusation Ps 75:6–7 trusted; equal souls, assigned stations; leveling left to the Magnificat's God; greatness as the towel
In marriage (Madon in Marriage) The Four Horsemen: prosecution, contempt from the bench, counter-suit, flight from court Soft startup, fondness-and-admiration, log-first responsibility, the closet before the kitchen
In the institution (<a class="wl" href="madon-and-the-anxious-tone-setter.html" title="Friedman found that institutions herd behind their most anxious member; Numbers 13–14 shows what that is theologically — an Accusing God evil report about providence given the gavel — and the parable of the talents prices it: safety-first is a verdict about the Master, the buried">Madon and the Anxious Tone-Setter) The anxious tone-setter with the gavel; the herd off the cliff; the gospel of niceness
Conflict procedure (The Speck and the Log / Riv) Tried in absentia on partial data; the measure self-filed Log first, then speck surgery; face to face, alone (Matt 18:15; Prov 25:8–10); riv-transfer for the rest
The body (Madon in Marriage / Shalom) "Rottenness in his bones" (Prov 12:4) — the immune finding "A tranquil heart gives life to the flesh" (Prov 14:30)
Emotional signature (Ingratitude / Shalom) The constant drip of discontentment (Prov 19:13) Joy — gladness under load; Ps 4:7 joy during a slander campaign
Sleep (Madon and Radical Egalitarianism / Shalom) The corner of the roof; the night sessions (Eph 4:26–27) "In peace I will both lie down and sleep" — during Absalom's coup (Ps 3:5; 4:8)
Exemplars Michal, Simon, Saul, Doeg, Korah, Absalom, Jezebel, Zeresh, the elder brother, Diotrephes Abigail, Jonathan, Caleb, Hannah, Ruth, Esther, Barnabas, the Baptist, Priscilla, Job (42:8–10)
Treatment of Christ Prosecuted him out of envy on data-defensible slander (Matt 26:59–61; 27:18) Is advocated by him — "I have prayed for you" (Luke 22:32)
Terminus (Ingratitude / Shalom) Hell: the false audit granted — a world with no benefits on file New creation: every account settled, the accuser crushed by the God of peace (Rom 16:20)

How to use this table

  • As the site map: every cell's concepts live on the linked home pages; this is the wiki read horizontally
  • As a diagnostic: pick a relationship — spouse, elder board, group chat, God — and read down the middle column asking which rows am I running here? The columns are not personality types; they are practices, switchable at the cross
  • As the sermon skeleton: any single row expands into a message (the row is the contrast structure); any three adjacent rows expand into a series
