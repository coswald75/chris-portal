Lexicon — The Wiki's Controlled Vocabulary

Part of Litigating Without a License. Every anchor phrase below is compressed doctrine: it has exactly one home page where it is derived from the text, and every other page uses it by linking to it. Say the concept the same way every time; link it at the phrase, in the flow of the prose. On the eventual website, each entry here is a hover-card.

The words themselves

Anchor phrase One-line definition Home
madon / midyanim Strife with a legal flavor — litigation-shaped contention, from the root din Madon
din The faculty of weighing, charging, pleading, ruling — licensed or curdled Din
riv The lawsuit word — a cause that must be carried to some bench Riv
diabolos / katēgoros The slanderer / the prosecuting attorney — the devil's method and office Diabolos — The Accuser
paraklētos / paraclesis The one called alongside who calls out — defense counsel; ordinary encouragement is the same word The Advocate (Paraklētos)
dibbah The "evil report": accurate observations carrying a false verdict (Num 13:32) Slander, Gossip, and the Busybody
allotriepiskopos Self-ordained overseer of another's household (1 Pet 4:15) Slander, Gossip, and the Busybody
hēsychia / the quiet spirit The closed prosecution — the biblical antonym of busybody and eshet midyanim alike Eshet Midyanim
eshet midyanim / eshet chayil Same grammar, opposite offices: woman of contentions, woman of valor Eshet Midyanim
ish midyanim The quarrelsome man — charcoal to embers (Prov 26:21) Ish Midyanim
antidikos The courtroom adversary — the widow's opponent and Peter's devil are the same word Eshet Midyanim
